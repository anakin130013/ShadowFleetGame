# Shadow Fleet: Quick Start

*Read this aloud at the table. Everything else, your phone teaches you as you play.*

---

## Set up (2 minutes)

1. **Plug in the table.** The round screen in the middle of the map shows a QR code.
2. **Everyone scans it** with their phone camera. No app to install.
3. **Pick a seat, then a character.** Tap a face to read their story. YOUR KIT shows what they're good at.
4. **First time playing?** Turn on **FIRST GAME** at the seat picker. Rivet, your droid, walks you around the real screens in round 1 and explains each new thing the first time it happens.
5. **On the map, put one of your claim tokens and all three of your fleet markers on your home world.** It's your hangar: you start holding it, with 3 ships in Fleet 1 (Fleets 2 and 3 are empty until you build ships for them). Then tap **START** on any phone.

---

## How to win

The game lasts **6, 8 or 10 rounds**. The most **Victory Points** at the end wins.

- **Hold worlds.** Outer Rim 2 VP · Mid Rim 3 · Core 5 · any home world 4 (a home world nobody plays counts as Outer Rim). Lose a world and its VP goes with it.
- **Finish your quest:** +5 VP. It's on your kit.
- **Destroy another player's fleet:** +2 VP.

---

## Every round: three steps

The bar at the top of your phone shows which step you're in. When you're done, **hold LOCK IN** on the BRIDGE button at the bottom. The next step starts when everyone has.

**1 · PREPARE (spend)**
Build or repair ships (2 Credits each), buy from the faction shops and the black market, and defend your worlds. You get income every round. Worlds next to yours tell you whose side they're on, so spreading out teaches you the map.

**2 · MOVEMENT (on the board)**
Move each of your fleets **one step** on the real map: to the next stop on its ring, or one lane in or out. Nebulae are stops too. Nothing to tap: the phone trusts you.

**3 · RESOLUTION (take worlds)**
Open the world your fleet is at on the **STAR MAP** and choose how. Each fleet acts once a round.

| | |
|---|---|
| **FIGHT** | Roll dice, ship against ship. The forecast shows your odds first. |
| **NEGOTIATE** | Answer the world's faction the way they want to hear it. |
| **BRIBE** | Pay Credits and it's yours. |

A world another player holds can be taken by **FIGHT**, an **UPRISING** (win over its people) or a **BUYOUT** (pay double; they can match it). Home worlds only fall to a fight, unless nobody plays that faction. You can also attack another player's fleet.

---

## Your pieces

- **Fleet markers 1, 2, 3** show where each of your fleets is. All three start on your home world, your hangar.
- **Claim tokens** show which worlds you hold. Take a world: put a token on it. Lose one: the new holder swaps in theirs.
- The phones keep the real count of ships and defenders, so the board only needs to show *where*.

---

## Good to know

- **Your secrets are on your phone.** Credits, favor, debts and jobs are yours alone. Talk, bluff and make deals across the table.
- **The galaxy remembers.** Four factions keep track of what you do to them. Favor opens their shops, brings their help, and earns gifts.
- **Check COMMS.** A red dot means trouble is coming. An amber dot means something needs you.
- **Once a game, use your power.** Your character has one big move, from round 3. Read it in YOUR KIT, then pick your moment.
- **Champions declare Eras.** Rise high enough with a faction and you can change the rules for everyone for 2 rounds.
- **The last round is the Final Hour.** Everyone secretly gives Last Orders: a target for each fleet, hired ships, a gambit. Then the strikes are revealed one at a time on the table.

---
