# Shadow Fleet: Assembly

*Printable versions: `ShadowFleet_Assembly_3Piece.pdf` and `ShadowFleet_Assembly_1Piece.pdf`.*

## You need

- **Print:** the map (3-piece: North half, South half, or 1-piece: the full map), the hub and the back plate. Print settings are saved in the 3MFs.
- **Pieces:** 9 world-claim tokens and fleet markers 1, 2, 3 for each faction, in its colour. Paint the engraved symbol or number, or colour-paint it in Bambu Studio.
- **The screen:** Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.43.
- **3 M2 self-tapping screws**, about 8 mm (6 to 10 mm works), and a straight USB-C cable.

**Install the firmware first,** while the screen is loose: open the web installer in Chrome, plug the screen in, click Install.

## Build it

1. **Join the halves** *(3-piece only).* Lay both halves face up and press them together along the seam until they click.
2. **Screen and plate into the hub.** Turn the hub over. Drop the screen in glass first, USB port toward the tunnel. Lay the plate on top and drive in the three screws.
3. **Snap the hub into the map.** Turn the hub glass side up and press it into the centre hole from the top until it clicks. Line the USB side up with its notch.
4. **Cable and play.** Plug the cable in through the tunnel and run it out to the edge of the map. Power it on and scan the code.
